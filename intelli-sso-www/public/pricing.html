FAQ

What is a Monthly Active User?

A distinct end-user identity (human end user, not a service account) that successfully completed at least one authentication in the calendar month. Refreshing an existing access token with a refresh token is not a new MAU. A user that signs in 500 times in a month counts as 1.

What counts against my tenant limit?

A tenant is a logical customer or business unit — one row in the tenants table. The default tenant on a fresh install counts. Deactivated tenants do not.

Do admin seats cost extra?

Not on Business or above — they are unlimited. Starter and Team tiers bundle a finite number of admin seats (see the table). Service-account tokens and scoped API keys are unlimited at every paid tier.

How is audit retention billed?

Within your tier's retention window it is included. Beyond the window, audit events are compressed and stored at $0.08/GB·month or exported to your own S3 / Snowflake / BigQuery at no extra charge.

Can I bring my own KMS for JWT signing?

Yes, on Cloud Dedicated and above. We can also integrate with AWS CloudHSM (add-on, $249/mo per tenant) on any paid tier.

Do you have a non-profit / education discount?

Yes — 50% off any cloud tier for verified non-profits, educational institutions and accredited open-source projects. Email support for the discount code before your first invoice.

Can I self-host and still buy support?

Yes — that is precisely the Self-Host Supported tier ($249/mo). You run the cluster; we answer the phone.

Is there a South African billing entity?

Yes. Invoices can be issued in USD, EUR or ZAR from the appropriate entity depending on your tax residency. VAT handling is per the rules of your billing country.
