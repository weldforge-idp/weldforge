This information powers your tenant provisioning. You'll receive your admin credentials, initial service-account token, and a link to the admin portal within a few minutes of checkout.

What happens after you click Your sign-up is recorded and the tenant name you choose is held for you straight away — that part does not depend on payment. Where card checkout is available for your billing region you go to the payment page next (we never see your card), and once payment confirms we create your tenant, mint a one-shot TENANT_ADMIN service-account token, and email you the credentials and a link to the admin portal — typically under two minutes. Sales-gated tiers always route to our sales inbox, with a reply within one business day to schedule a short onboarding call.
